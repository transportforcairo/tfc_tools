about=TfC Tools is a QGIS plugin suite developed by Transport for Cairo (TfC) to streamline and standardise transport data processing workflows.
    The suite provides user-friendly interfaces for common analytical tasks used in TfC’s research and transport data management projects.
    TfC Tools currently includes several tools organised into two groups:
    1. RouteLab Tools: tools that migrate field survey data from TfC’s RouteLab database into standardised tables, either in GeoPackage format or in a PostGIS Spatial Data Infrastructure (SDI).
       a. Export RouteLab to GeoPackage
       b. Export SDI (PostGIS) to GeoPackage
       c. RL2SDI (RouteLab to SDI Migration)
    2. GIS Tools: tools for transport network analysis, GTFS generation, and transport metrics.
       a. GIS2GTFS – converts GIS data into GTFS.
       b. Refresh SDI derived layers – after manual GIS edits
       c. Trip and Route Revenue Estimator
       d. Vehicle and Passenger Flow Estimator
    User Guide (PDF): https://github.com/transportforcairo/tfc_tools/blob/main/tfc_tools_user_guide.pdf
